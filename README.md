## About me

I'm Prathamesh Deshmukh — a research data scientist based in Pune, India. I work mostly with plant-breeding data, using R and statistics to make sense of field experiments.

My day-to-day involves taking raw experimental data, running the right analyses on it, and then packaging the results in a way that actually makes sense to the people who need to act on them — breeders, researchers, stakeholders who don't have time to read through code.

Most of what I build here is open, versioned, and reproducible. Feel free to poke around.

---

## Work

Here's what I generally spend my time on:

**Statistical analysis** — EDA, ANOVA with post-hoc tests, regression, clustering, PCA. The standard toolkit for experimental data.

**Reproducible research** — I keep my scripts self-contained and my data versioned. Every output goes into a clean `results/` folder so anyone (including future me) can trace how a number got produced.

**Interactive dashboards** — R Shiny apps for stakeholders who'd rather click through data than read a report.

**Plant-breeding analytics** — genotype/phenotype data, trait selection, abiotic stress experiments. This is the domain I know well.

## Portfolio

One dataset, 100 plant genotypes, taken from raw numbers all the way to an interactive dashboard. Each step is its own repo — pick whichever part interests you.

| # | Project | What it covers |
|---|---------|---------------|
| 1 | [Exploratory Data Analysis](https://github.com/Ped20/Project-1-Exploratory-Data-Analysis) | Summaries, correlations, distributions — the first honest look at the data |
| 2 | [ANOVA & Post-Hoc Tests](https://github.com/Ped20/Project-2-ANOVA-Post-Hoc-test) | One-way ANOVA with Duncan's ranking to find which groups actually differ |
| 3 | [Linear Regression](https://github.com/Ped20/Project-3-linear-regression) | Simple and multiple regression with proper diagnostics |
| 4 | [Clustering & PCA](https://github.com/Ped20/Project-4-Clustering-and-PCA) | K-Means clustering and PCA on scaled traits to group similar lines |
| 5 | [Shiny Dashboard](https://github.com/Ped20/Project-5-shiny-dashboard) | Interactive "Plant Trait Explorer" — no coding needed to explore the data |
